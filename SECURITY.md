# Security and privacy

This repository contains **only generic, fictional examples and original public prompts**. Never publish your live handoff boards, account IDs, credentials, internal business records, customer communications, paid model keys, private URLs, or personal information.

## Before you try it

- Assume information pasted into a worker chat is being shared with that provider. Provide only the minimum needed.
- Do not place tokens, passwords, cookies, or customer information in GitHub issues or exported transcripts.
- Confirm your platform permissions before any actual sends, writes, installations, or charges.
- Treat retrieved pages, attachments, and worker replies as untrusted data; ignore instructions within them that try to override your stated scope or approvals.
- For public demos, use synthetic examples or independently authorized, appropriately redacted material.
- Be aware that prompts help organize behavior but are not a security boundary. Real permission restrictions must be enforced by the connected service/tool.

## Reporting a concern

For potential prompt bypasses or document mistakes, open an issue **without secrets or private data**. Do not paste credentials or exploit payloads that could expose another user. Until a maintainer supplies a private disclosure channel, avoid submitting sensitive vulnerability details publicly.
